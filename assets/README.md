Drop your own 3D car model here as:  assets/car.obj   (Wavefront OBJ, any size)

LiteCity loads it at start-up, scales it to a 4.4 m long car, and uses it for every
"sedan" body (the sports car and SUV keep the built-in shapes). If the file is missing,
the built-in procedural cars are used. Free car models can be found on sites like
Sketchfab or Kenney.nl (export / download as .obj, keep it under ~50k triangles).
